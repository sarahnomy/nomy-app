Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Nomy is a self-reflection and wellbeing app for late-diagnosed autistic adults. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can ask us about your data.

What Nomy is not

Nomy is not a medical, diagnostic, therapy, emergency, or crisis service. The app offers reflection prompts, emotional language, communication support, and grounding tools. It does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure any condition.

Information you give us

When you use Nomy, you may choose to provide:

  • account information, such as username and email address;
  • reflections, check-ins, and written responses you save;
  • answers you choose in Emotionize, Check-in, Express, and Toolkit;
  • messages you send if you contact support.

Information stored on your device

Some reflections and app activity may be stored locally on your device so the app can remember your progress, recent activity, and saved entries. If you delete the app or clear local storage, locally stored data may be removed.

Information stored in your account

If you create an account and save entries while logged in, Nomy may store those entries on our server so they can appear in your Profile and be connected to your account.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • provide the app features you choose to use;
  • save and show your reflections, check-ins, and patterns;
  • keep your account working;
  • improve app safety, reliability, and usability;
  • respond to support requests.

Third-party services

Nomy may use trusted service providers for hosting, app builds, and app distribution. If AI-generated support is enabled, text you submit for that feature may be sent to an AI provider only to generate the response you requested.

Subscriptions and community safety

Apple processes iPhone subscription payments and receives an opaque NOMY account token. NOMY verifies purchases directly with Apple and stores subscription transaction identifiers and status to manage and restore premium access. NOMY does not receive your payment card details.

Stories submitted for review, report reasons and details, and blocked-account records are stored on our backend. Moderators can review Stories and reports. Blocked authors are not notified. An anonymous Story does not display your account username.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information when needed to run Nomy, comply with the law, protect safety, or provide a feature you ask to use.

Data deletion

You can delete your account inside NOMY in Settings. Account deletion removes your account, Stories, reflections, reports and blocking records, and invalidates your sessions. Stored audio and the subscription service’s customer record are queued for deletion with retries if a service is temporarily unavailable. Apple may retain purchase records under its policies. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage it in your Apple account settings. You can also contact hello@nomy.app for data requests.

Children

Nomy is designed for adults and is not intended for children.

Contact

For privacy questions, support, or deletion requests, contact: hello@nomy.app
